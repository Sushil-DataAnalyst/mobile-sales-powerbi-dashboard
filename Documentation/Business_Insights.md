# 💡 Mobile Sales Dashboard — Business Insights

The following observations are based on the values and visuals visible in the dashboard screenshots. They describe the **default dashboard view**; interactive filters can change the results.

## 1. Overall Sales Performance

**Finding:** Total sales are approximately **₹769.20M**.

**Evidence:** The executive KPI card displays `769M`, while the detailed total in the report table is approximately `₹769.20M`.

**Business implication:** The KPI provides management with an immediate view of overall sales scale before drilling into products, geography, payment methods, or time periods.

## 2. Transaction & Quantity Overview

**Finding:** The dashboard displays approximately **19K units** and **3.835K transactions**.

**Evidence:** The headline KPI cards show `19K` for Total Quantity and `3.835K` for Count of Transaction ID.

**Business implication:** These metrics can be monitored together to understand transaction volume and sales activity.

## 3. Year-wise Performance

**Finding:** The year comparison shows a major increase from 2021 to 2022, followed by high sales in 2023 and a lower displayed total in 2024.

**Evidence:** The year-wise Total Sales visual shows 2022 as the highest displayed bar, with 2023 also high and 2024 lower.

**Business implication:** Management can investigate the drivers behind the change in annual performance and use the same-period comparison to identify improvement or decline.

## 4. Same Period Last Year Comparison

**Finding:** The dashboard directly compares Total Sales with Same Period Last Year at year, quarter, and month levels.

**Evidence:** The report contains comparison tables and clustered column charts using `Total_Sales` and `Same Period Last Year`.

**Business implication:** This supports faster identification of periods performing above or below the previous-year benchmark.

## 5. Mobile Model Performance

**Finding:** The displayed model chart shows several models contributing around ₹50M+ each, with **iPhone SE at approximately ₹60M** among the strongest displayed performers.

**Evidence:** The mobile-model visual labels iPhone SE around `60M`, OnePlus Nord around `58M`, and a Galaxy Note model around `56M`.

**Business implication:** Product-level performance can help management prioritize high-contributing models and investigate weaker performers.

## 6. Monthly Quantity Trend

**Finding:** Quantity varies across months rather than remaining constant.

**Evidence:** The monthly line chart shows visible peaks and troughs, with July around `1,700` units in the displayed view and February around `1,451`.

**Business implication:** Monthly fluctuations can support inventory planning, campaign timing, and investigation of seasonal demand patterns.

## 7. Payment Method Distribution

**Finding:** Transactions are distributed across UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, and Cash, with no single method overwhelmingly dominating the displayed view.

**Evidence:** The payment-method donut chart shows approximately 24–26% for each displayed payment category.

**Business implication:** The business can monitor payment preferences and ensure reporting covers all major transaction channels.

## 8. Customer Rating Status

**Finding:** The dashboard separates customer ratings into Good, Average, and Poor categories, with the Good category visibly largest in the displayed view.

**Evidence:** The Rating Status visual shows the Good bar extending substantially farther than Average and Poor.

**Business implication:** Management can monitor customer satisfaction alongside sales performance rather than evaluating revenue in isolation.

## 9. Geographic Analysis

**Finding:** Sales vary across the cities shown on the map.

**Evidence:** The map visual plots city-level sales using different bubble sizes.

**Business implication:** Geographic analysis can help identify stronger markets and areas that may require additional sales or marketing attention.

## 10. Day-wise Sales Pattern

**Finding:** Sales vary across days of the week in the displayed analysis.

**Evidence:** The day-wise visual shows values ranging roughly from `₹105M` to `₹115M` across the displayed days.

**Business implication:** Day-level patterns can help evaluate weekly demand behavior and support campaign or staffing decisions.

---

## 📌 Analyst Takeaway

The strongest feature of this report is the ability to move from **high-level KPIs → time comparison → product performance → geography → customer ratings → payment behavior** within one interactive Power BI solution.

This makes the project relevant to practical **MIS reporting, Data Analyst, and Business Intelligence** workflows where the objective is not only to present data, but to help stakeholders investigate performance and make informed decisions.
