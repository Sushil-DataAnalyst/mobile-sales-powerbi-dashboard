# Business Insights

This document will contain the final business insights from the Mobile Sales dashboard.

The observations should be based on the final dashboard/data rather than generic assumptions. After the final PBIX and source data are uploaded, document:

- Overall sales performance
- Year-over-year movement
- Best-performing mobile models
- City-wise sales patterns
- Monthly and quarterly trends
- Payment method distribution
- Customer rating distribution
- Day-wise performance

## Final Insight Format

For each insight, use:

**Finding → Evidence → Business implication**

This keeps the project focused on business problem-solving rather than dashboard design alone.
